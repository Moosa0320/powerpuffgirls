# Question bank for the agricultural expert

## Yellowing (patta peela)
- Did the yellowing start on old (lower) leaves or new (top) leaves?
- Is it the whole leaf, between the veins, or at the edges?
- Was any fertilizer amount changed in the 14 days before?

## Wilting (murjhana)
- Do plants recover in the evening or stay wilted?
- Was the soil very wet or very dry when wilting started?
- Is wilting on single plants or in patches?

## Spots (dhabbe)
- What colour are the spots and do they have a ring or edge?
- Did spots appear after rain or after a spray?
- Are spots on leaves only, or also on stems and fruit?

## Stunting (barhwar ruk jana)
- Is the whole field small or only patches?
- How did this field compare to neighbouring fields at the same age?
- Were seed, variety or planting date different this season?

## Always
- Has the symptom spread since it was first seen?
- Can the expert see the field or recent photos before any spray?
